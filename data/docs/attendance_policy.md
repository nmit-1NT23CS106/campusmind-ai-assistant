# Attendance Policy

## Minimum attendance requirement
Students must attend at least 75% of the classes held in each course to be eligible for the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated as classes attended divided by classes held, up to the last working day of the semester.

## Condonation of shortage
Students with attendance between 65% and 74.99% in a course may apply for condonation to the Head of Department. The application needs supporting documents, such as a medical certificate or an approved event letter, and must be submitted within 5 working days after the last working day. A condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course applies. Condonation can be granted at most twice during the entire degree programme.

## Detention
A student whose attendance is below 65% in a course is detained in that course. The student cannot sit the end-semester examination for it and must re-register for the course when it is next offered.

## Medical and on-duty leave
Medical leave requires a doctor's certificate submitted within 7 days of returning to class. On-duty leave for approved university events such as sports, cultural and technical fests is counted as present, up to a maximum of 10% of the classes held in a course.

## Late arrival and publishing
A student who arrives more than 15 minutes after a class begins is marked absent for that class. Faculty must publish attendance every Friday so that students can check their records.
