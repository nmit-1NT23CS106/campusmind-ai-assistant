# Grading Rules

## Course marks breakdown
Every theory course is assessed out of 100 marks: 40 marks for internal assessment and 60 marks for the end-semester examination.

### Internal assessment
The 40 internal marks are made up of two internal tests, IA1 and IA2, worth 15 marks each, plus assignments worth 10 marks.

## Passing criteria
A student passes a course by scoring at least 40 marks out of 100 overall and at least 21 marks out of 60 in the end-semester examination. Failing either condition results in an F grade.

## Grade scale
| Marks | Grade | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| 90 to 100 | O | 10 |
| 80 to 89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70 to 79 | A | 8 |
| 60 to 69 | B+ | 7 |
| 50 to 59 | B | 6 |
| 40 to 49 | C | 5 |
| Below 40 | F | 0 |

## Calculating SGPA and CGPA
SGPA is the sum of credits multiplied by grade points for the semester's courses, divided by the total credits of that semester. CGPA is calculated the same way across all semesters completed. A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to graduate.

## Make-up for missed internal tests
A student who misses an internal test because of illness, with a valid medical certificate, may take one make-up test within 10 days. No re-test is allowed for any other reason.
