# Academic Calendar: Odd Semester 2026

## Semester dates
Classes commence on Monday, 3 August 2026. The last working day of the semester is Friday, 27 November 2026. Course registration closes on 14 August 2026, and late registration costs Rs. 200 per day.

## Internal tests and assignments
The first internal test, IA1, is held from 14 to 18 September 2026. The second internal test, IA2, is held from 26 to 30 October 2026. The last date to submit assignments is 13 November 2026.

## End-semester examinations
Practical examinations run from 30 November to 4 December 2026. Theory examinations begin on Monday, 7 December 2026.

## Holidays and breaks
Gandhi Jayanti on 2 October and Dussehra on 20 October are holidays. The Deepavali break runs from 7 to 10 November 2026.
