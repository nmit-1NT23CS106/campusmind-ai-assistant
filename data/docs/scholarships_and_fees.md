# Scholarships and Fees

## Tuition fee payment
Tuition for the odd semester is due by 15 August. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies for up to 15 days. After 15 days of delay, the student cannot attend classes until all dues are cleared.

## Merit scholarship
Students with a CGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous year receive a 25% tuition waiver, and those with a CGPA from 8.5 to 8.99 receive a 10% waiver. The scholarship is renewed every year and requires at least 85% overall attendance.

## Need-based financial aid
Students whose family income is below Rs. 4 lakh per year can apply by 31 August with an income certificate. The aid covers up to 50% of tuition.

## Refund policy
A student who withdraws before classes commence receives a 90% refund of tuition. Withdrawal within 15 days after classes commence earns a 50% refund. After that, no refund is given.
