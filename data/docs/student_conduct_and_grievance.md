# Student Conduct and Grievance Redressal

## Anti-ragging
The university has zero tolerance for ragging. Complaints go to the Anti-Ragging Committee, which can act within 24 hours. Punishments include suspension, expulsion and a police complaint.

## Grievance redressal
Students can submit a written complaint to the Student Grievance Cell in Room 12 of the Admin Block or through the student portal. The cell acknowledges a complaint within 2 working days and resolves it within 15 working days. A student who is not satisfied can appeal to the Dean of Student Affairs within 7 days.

## Lost ID cards
A lost ID card can be replaced at the Admin Office for a fee of Rs. 300.

## Mobile phones in class
Mobile phones must be switched off or kept on silent in classrooms and laboratories. Faculty may ask a student to deposit the phone for the duration of the class.
