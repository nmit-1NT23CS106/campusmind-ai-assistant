# Examination Regulations

## Eligibility and hall ticket
Only students who meet the attendance requirement, or hold an approved condonation, and who have cleared all fees receive a hall ticket. Hall tickets are issued by the department three days before the examinations begin.

## Exam day rules
Candidates must report 30 minutes before the exam starts and carry the university ID card and the hall ticket. Mobile phones, smartwatches and any other electronic devices are not allowed in the hall. No candidate is admitted after 30 minutes from the start of the paper.

## Malpractice
Using unfair means leads to cancellation of that paper. Repeated or serious cases lead to cancellation of all papers of the semester and may result in suspension for one year.

## Supplementary examinations
A student who fails a course can reappear in the next supplementary examination. A student gets at most 3 attempts per course. The fee is Rs. 1000 per paper.

## Results and revaluation
Results are declared within 30 days of the last examination. A student may apply for revaluation within 5 days of the result declaration by paying Rs. 800 per paper. The outcome is announced within 15 days, and revised marks replace the original marks only if they are higher.
