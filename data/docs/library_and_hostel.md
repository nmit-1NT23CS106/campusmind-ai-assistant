# Library and Hostel Rules

## Library borrowing limits
Undergraduate students may borrow 4 books for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow 6 books for 21 days. A book can be renewed twice if no other student has reserved it.

## Library fines
The fine for an overdue book is Rs. 2 per day per book. For a lost book, the student pays the replacement cost plus Rs. 200 as a processing charge.

## Library hours
The library is open from 8:30 am to 8:00 pm, Monday to Saturday, and stays open until midnight during examination weeks. It is closed on Sundays.

## Hostel rules
First-year residents must be back in the hostel by 9:30 pm, and all other residents by 10:30 pm. Outings on weekdays need the warden's permission. Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between 4 pm and 6 pm on Sundays.

## Hostel fees
The hostel fee, including mess charges, is Rs. 65,000 per year. A refundable caution deposit of Rs. 10,000 is collected at admission.
